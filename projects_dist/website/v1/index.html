Why this website?

For a reason or another I can't publish all my games in just one platform. If I join other socials I'll add them to the social tab, and if I have any news/FAQ I can put them here; but more than anything I needed a place I could treat as my "internet home".

How did I start programming?

I started programming just after my art school graduation. Never seen a line of code before that moment, I thought it was some kind of unapprociable science to me. Yet, I slowly grew close to the idea of being able to express myself through it and that gave me the push I needed to learn more to be able to do more.
